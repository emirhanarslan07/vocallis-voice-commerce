# Vocallis Voice Commerce

Create a high-converting, modern SaaS landing page for "Vocallis" — the 24/7 AI Voice Sales & Instant Callback Platform for E-Commerce and Online Brands.

Core Value Proposition:
"Turn Website Visitors Into High-Paying Customers With Real-Time Voice AI."
Traditional text chatbots get ignored. Vocallis replaces boring text widgets with an instant, human-like AI voice sales representative that answers product questions, looks up orders, and calls back hot leads within 60 seconds.

Key Sections to Build:
1. Header / Navbar:
   - Logo: Vocallis
   - Navigation: Features, Interactive Demo, How It Works, Pricing
   - CTA Button: "Start Free Trial" & "Book a Demo"

2. Hero Section:
   - Tagline / Badge: "THE #1 VOICE AI CONVERSION ENGINE FOR MODERN STORES"
   - Main Headline: "Call Back Every Lead in 60 Seconds & Close Sales on Autopilot."
   - Subheadline: "Replace lifeless text chatbots. When a high-intent shopper leaves their number or asks a question, Vocallis AI calls them in under 60 seconds to answer questions, recommend products, and close the order."
   - Dual CTAs: Primary "Start 14-Day Free Trial", Secondary "Try Interactive Voice Demo"
   - Social Proof Bar: "Works seamlessly with Shopify, WooCommerce, Webflow, and Custom Stores" with platform logos.

3. Live Interactive Widget Simulator (Centerpiece Component):
   - A realistic e-commerce product mockup (e.g., luxury watch or modern furniture) with an embedded Vocallis widget.
   - Interactive Tabs:
     a) "60-Second Lead Callback": User enters a sample phone number and sees a live timer counting down from 60s with an animated incoming phone call preview.
     b) "Live Voice Product Advisor": A clickable microphone button showing dynamic voice waveform and an AI transcript answering sizing/specs questions.
     c) "Instant Order Status": Customer asks "Where is my order #1042?" and AI instantly displays tracking & delivery time.

4. 3 Core Pillars (Feature Grid):
   - Pillar 1: "Sub-60 Second Speed to Lead" — Harvard Business Review proves calling within 5 minutes increases sales conversion by 21x. Vocallis does it in 45 seconds while the shopper is still on your page.
   - Pillar 2: "In-Browser Voice Shopping Assistant" — Shoppers speak naturally to ask about sizing, material, compatibility, and discounts instead of reading lengthy FAQs.
   - Pillar 3: "Automated WISMO & Support Resolution" — Resolves 60% of repetitive 'Where is my order?' inquiries automatically via direct Shopify integration.

5. How It Works (Simple 3-Step Walkthrough):
   - Step 1: Install with 1 Click (Shopify app or 1 line of JavaScript script tag).
   - Step 2: Upload your product catalog & brand tone in 5 minutes.
   - Step 3: Watch your visitor-to-buyer conversion jump by 35%+.

6. Transparent SaaS Pricing (Self-Serve Friendly):
   - Starter ($49/month): Up to 150 voice minutes, In-browser Voice Widget, Basic order tracking.
   - Growth ($149/month) [Most Popular]: Up to 600 voice minutes, 60-Second Instant Callbacks, Full Shopify Sync, Custom Voice & Tone.
   - Scale ($299/month): Unlimited voice minutes, Priority routing, Dedicated outbound phone number, CRM & Webhook integrations.

7. Final CTA Banner:
   - "Stop Losing 97% of Your Store Visitors. Activate Vocallis Today."
   - Input field: "Enter your store URL" + "Get Instant Demo"

8. Clean, modern SaaS footer with compliance badges (GDPR ready, CAN-SPAM, TCPA compliant opt-in safeguards).

Style & Vibe:
Sleek, dark or clean minimalist tech aesthetic, smooth micro-interactions, high-contrast CTA buttons, crisp typography, and high-trust enterprise finish.
